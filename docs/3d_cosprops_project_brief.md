# Project Brief: 3D CosProps Redesign

## 1. Project Overview
**Client:** 3D CosProps
**Objective:** Redesign the existing 3D modeling and printing studio website into a high-fidelity, professional digital experience that reflects the "master craftsman" quality of their physical work.
**Design System:** Forge & Fidelity

## 2. Brand Identity & Aesthetic
The brand identity has been shifted from a generic hobbyist look to a premium, technical, and immersive studio aesthetic.
- **Core Theme:** Cinematic, technical, and high-fidelity.
- **Color Palette:** 
    - Primary: **Metallic Gold (#d4af37)** for highlights and CTAs.
    - Background: **Deep Obsidian / Dark Mode** for a premium "dark workshop" feel.
    - Accents: Subtle grayscale textures (e.g., dragon scale/carbon fiber patterns).
- **Typography:** 
    - Primary Font: **Space Grotesk** (Clean, technical, modern).
    - Styling: Heavy use of uppercase, tracking, and italics for brand headers.

## 3. Site Architecture
The site is structured to guide users from inspiration to acquisition:
1. **Home:** High-impact hero section with cinematic visuals and direct paths to services or commissions.
2. **Services:** Overview of 3D modeling, printing, and finishing capabilities.
3. **Projects (Portfolio):** A visual showcase of completed work with filtering options.
4. **Shop (The Armory):** E-commerce grid for physical kits, finished props, and digital STL files.
5. **Commissions:** Dedicated inquiry form for custom, high-end prop projects.

## 4. Key Design Principles
- **Immersive Visuals:** Use of large, high-quality imagery and background textures to create depth.
- **Unified Navigation:** A persistent, high-blur navigation bar with consistent links across all views.
- **Technical Precision:** Clean borders, sharp corners (Round Four), and purposeful whitespace to mirror the precision of 3D printing.
- **Call to Action (CTA) Clarity:** High-contrast gold buttons to drive user conversion toward commissions and shop sales.

## 5. Technical Stack
- **Frontend:** Responsive HTML5 / Tailwind CSS.
- **Assets:** Optimized raster imagery for prop showcases and vector-based typography for the logo.
- **Device Support:** Initial rollout focused on Desktop (1440px), with a responsive mobile strategy in place.
